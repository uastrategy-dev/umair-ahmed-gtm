THE GOAL
An editorial, print-inspired, art-directed personal site for a senior GTM operator. Think: beautifully typeset magazine dossier meets cinematic dusk landscape. It must NOT look like an AI-generated portfolio or a SaaS template.

DESIGN TOKENS
Colors (keep my existing brand palette, use it with more confidence):
- Dusk navy #1b2c4a (primary dark surface + primary text on light)
- Deep navy variant #223a5e (raised surfaces on dark)
- Cream #F4E8CF (light surface + text on dark)

Typography (load from Google Fonts):

- Body/labels: "DM Sans" (400/500/600). Body 17-18px, line-height 1.6.
- Micro-labels/eyebrows: DM Sans 11-12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.18em, gold or muted — OR a monospace (IBM Plex Mono) for section numbers and data labels to add technical texture. Pick monospace for eyebrows and stat labels.
